Confidence Interval: statistic ± margin of error

In your own words, explain what each part of the confidence interval equation means. Make sure you explain what the statistic represents and what the margin of error tells us.

Hypothesis Test: p-value ≤ α → reject H₀
p-value > α → fail to reject H₀

In your own words, explain how a significance test leads to a decision. Draw out the process from the null and alternative hypotheses, to the test statistic and p-value, to the final decision: reject the null hypothesis or fail to reject the null hypothesis. Make it clear how the p-value is compared to the significance level, α.

Finally, include a quick explanation of Type I and Type II errors. A Type I error happens when we reject the null hypothesis even though it is actually true. A Type II error happens when we fail to reject the null hypothesis even though it is actually false.
